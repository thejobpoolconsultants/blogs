# Research content audit

Audit date: 20 September 2026. Scope: all source pages, shared components, quote data, article metadata and bodies, writing templates and editorial guidance. The publication contains nine published articles, one unpublished draft and 70 generated HTML pages. Keyword inventory: `artifacts/research-language-audit.txt`. This is an editorial and software review, not an independent reproduction of any cited research.

**Never make the explanation stronger than the evidence.**

## Findings

No fabricated benchmark measurements or claims that TheJobPoolConsultants conducted the cited research were found in the published article text. Existing attention, architecture and context articles already distinguished proposed designs or experiments from results. The main weaknesses were inconsistent source roles, missing automatic disclosures, an undersourced contextual-embedding explanation, and a research template that incorrectly defaulted to a generic explainer.

The initial suspicion about the tutorial's common-word query was disproved by executing the snippet: “the” occurs in the agents sample and returns that document. Its original example is retained. Running this small code example is a tutorial check, not validation of an academic paper.

## Pages and public language reviewed

- Homepage: research introduction now says “Research papers, without the wall of equations.” Supporting copy explicitly asks what the authors tested and what their evidence supports. The fixed context quote and other signature lines remain.
- `/research/` and `/topics/research/`: named Research Papers & Insights; explain the publication's role and include “How we cover research.” The short navigation label remains Research.
- About: foregrounds the evidence rule and explains the publication's research coverage.
- Research feature and Paper → Product series: genuine paper title/authors/date and original-paper link remain. Neither claims publication research.
- Article index/cards, article headers, search types and results: use human-readable research format names while keeping machine filter IDs stable. Available formats include Paper Explained, Research Map and Engineering Interpretation.
- Topic, tag, learning, evolution, landscape and remaining hub templates: reviewed keyword matches and generic research references. No claims of publication-conducted studies were found; no gratuitous disclaimers added.
- Source groups and article metadata: every source classified; primary papers distinguished from supporting documentation and textbooks. Each source has a human-readable evidence scope.
- Templates: all six accept the new source schema. The research template uses the research topic and Paper → Product type, separates the requested sections and demonstrates statement labels. Original Research, Experiment Results, Reproduction Study and Independent Validation remain reserved, unsupported types pending a documented future workflow.

## System changes and publication gate

`research-editorial.ts` centralizes copy, content types, labels and the paper-based predicate. `ResearchStatement.astro` provides six semantic variants: source-backed summary, engineering interpretation, illustrative example, proposed experiment, editorial perspective and evidence limitation. Source summaries require a link and label. Proposed experiments automatically state that no results are claimed.

`ResearchEditorialNote.astro` renders automatically near the sources when an article has a paper type, the research topic or a cited paper. It currently appears on attention, RAG, production RAG, context and NLP: five articles. The four documentation/textbook-only articles do not receive a misleading research disclaimer. There are 18 classified statement blocks across the published articles.

Schema validation requires explicit source role, kind and scope, and a primary paper for published paper-based content. The new build-time research gate checks rendered source groups, disclosures, links, the preserved attention title and experiment statements, and high-risk claim phrases. It tests the classification rules for future paper types. The human quality checklist in `EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md` covers the semantic judgments automation cannot settle.

## Verification and remaining risks

Build, typecheck, lint, formatting and browser results are recorded in `RESEARCH_VERIFICATION.md`. The tutorial's three existing assertions and additional “the,” “a,” and paraphrase queries passed locally; this is code verification only. No paper experiment was reproduced and no independent scientific validation is claimed.

Remaining editorial risks: source extraction and citation review are not a full replication or exhaustive scientific review; external pages may change; the textbook was checked through indexed official content after direct extraction failed; marketing statements still need contextual judgment; automated phrase checks cannot establish truth. Future original research requires documented methods, evidence and a changed publication workflow. The unpublished overfitting example still needs execution and review before release.

No commit, push or deployment was performed. Existing modified LICENSE/README and untracked site files remain in the working tree.
