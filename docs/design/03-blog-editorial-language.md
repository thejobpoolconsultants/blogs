# Publication editorial language

TheJobPoolConsultants is the only visible organization name. `Brand.astro` follows the sibling application's active text wordmark; its descriptor is `AI · ENGINEERING · RESEARCH`. The favicon is the sibling application's arrow-and-gold-point SVG, with its accessible title expanded to the full organization name. Header, mobile navigation and footer offer a main-website return link from `siteConfig.mainSite`.

Gold signals human insight: the hero's italic “what’s next,” eyebrow marks, KeyIdea notes, research labels and the final context statement. Cyan signals systems: data connections, maps, flows, learning sequences and technical links. Keep these roles stable rather than giving each page a new palette.

The homepage alternates an open hero, a navy architecture feature, six subject cards, a learning timeline, conceptual artwork, numbered mechanism essays, architecture, research/build columns, ordered learning previews and a full-width editorial statement. This is a publication, not a metrics dashboard. There are no invented subscribers, progress meters or article counts.

`KeyIdea.astro` uses a gold rule and label without a filled panel. `MentalModel.astro` uses the same reading rhythm with cyan and a compact explanation. Notes summarize the existing article's technical idea and should not become promotional claims.

Research presents the original paper separately from our explanation. Metadata for _Attention Is All You Need_ was checked against [the primary arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762). Its eight authors, 2017 submission year and identifier are displayed; no unverified venue, citation count or impact metric is added. Its explanation remains the existing published article. Topic searches may have no dedicated article yet, which the interface states explicitly.

Social previews use the same midnight/ivory/gold palette and exact organization name. The complete original content taxonomy, routes, authors, draft rules, feed, metadata and article strategy remain intact.
