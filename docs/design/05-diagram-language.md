# Diagram language

Three repeated motifs connect the publication:

1. Intelligence line: thin cyan connections in maps, model diagrams, and learning sequences.
2. Insight mark: a small gold point/rule at human inputs, key ideas, and research labels.
3. Knowledge coordinates: restrained monospace labels for figure, topic and concept context.

Architecture diagrams distinguish human intent (gold), system steps (cyan), evaluation (teal), and neutral infrastructure. Every node has a text label. The agent diagram explicitly separates intent/context, model plus orchestration, tools/state/evaluation, and the feedback loop. The RAG variant shows evidence, access rules and generation; it does not suggest that retrieval guarantees correctness.

The homepage map is an SVG group of real topic links. Keyboard focus and pointer hover reveal a short explanation; activation follows the topic. All links have accessible names. There is no continuously moving background or autoplay. Without JavaScript the map remains a useful linked diagram with its default caption.

Article thumbnails are decorative SVGs with `aria-hidden`; the adjacent title and summary convey their meaning. Their structures correspond to ML curves, neural layers, token vectors, attention weights, retrieval pipelines, context stacks, or agent loops. Research artwork is a symbolic attention motif, not a reproduction of a paper figure or a measured result.

Figures in article content include captions. Concept progressions state that methods coexist and are not replacements for one another. CSS-only hover response is disabled under reduced motion.
