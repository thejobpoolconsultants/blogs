# Content strategy

Discovery → Curiosity → Understanding → Deep dive → Learning path → Build → Return.

Maintain a balanced library across all 15 topic areas. A subject can exist in the taxonomy before it has published articles; show truthful empty states. The initial release contains nine evergreen pieces rather than a fabricated backlog of finished posts.

Organize four independent concepts: topic, subtopic, ordered series, and article. Learning paths connect available articles to an ordered concept roadmap. A planned stage is not a completed lesson.

Recurring formats: AI in 5 Minutes, Under the Hood, The Architecture, Paper → Product, Model Shift, Build This, and One Big Idea. A format is useful only when it suits the material.

Each article should connect to a next idea, a series, and a relevant path when appropriate. Keep recent developments separate from evergreen explanations; use news-analysis for time-sensitive pieces.

## Repurposing

After editorial review, derive a short social post, a thread, a diagram, quote cards, a 60-second summary, and a newsletter paragraph. Check each for lost nuance. Never auto-post. Social preview PNGs are generated automatically; wider campaign assets are an editorial workflow.

## Expansion priority

Publish additional foundations and evaluation material before filling every advanced topic. Add dedicated lessons to paths in prerequisite order. The suggested 50-article backlog is recorded in docs/content/backlog.md. It is planning material, not a publication schedule.

Newsletter and analytics are absent by default. Add a real provider and privacy documentation before enabling either.
