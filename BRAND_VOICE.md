# TheJobPoolConsultants brand voice

Always spell the organization name **TheJobPoolConsultants**. Machine identifiers and the existing GitHub account URL use their required lowercase spelling; visible copy uses the exact organization name.

Simple, not simplistic. Technical, not unnecessarily academic. Confident, not exaggerated. Visual, not cluttered. Curious, not clickbait. Practical, not theoretical-only. Forward-looking, but evidence-based.

Begin with the change, question, or problem. Introduce the technology after establishing why it matters.

Prefer “A chatbot answers once. An agent has to decide what happens next.” over an unexplained framework definition. Prefer specific limits and mechanisms over sweeping predictions.

Use the positioning “Understand intelligence. Build what’s next.” sparingly. Name sources, distinguish interpretation, and leave readers with a useful next question. Never imply guaranteed career outcomes, universal research conclusions, or human-like model understanding.

## Editorial statements

Use `src/data/editorial-quotes.ts` as the shared statement library. The homepage has one deliberately selected One Big Idea; never choose it randomly or rotate it on refresh. See [the editorial statement report](docs/design/12-editorial-statements.md) for the placement map and retained signature lines.

Prefer one or two short sentences, usually 6-20 words per sentence. Contrast, reframing, progression and capacity-versus-design can clarify an engineering idea. Keep a shorter line when it earns its place. Review every statement for technical accuracy and useful context; reject hype, vague motivation, claims of guaranteed outcomes and language that implies human-like understanding.

Use `EditorialStatement.astro` for the existing feature band or inline section copy, and `TopicInsight.astro` for a smaller topic or article transition. These are publication statements: no decorative quotation marks, fake attribution, celebrity names or borrowed campaign slogans. Keep real research quotations and citations separate.

Retain the established agent, RAG, learning-order, context-design and representation lines. Use approximately one to three statements in a long article only when they clarify an argument. Never add them automatically through the article template.

The footer signature is “Curiosity starts the question. Evidence earns the answer.” The About-page philosophy is “Ask better questions. Build better systems. Understand what comes next.”
